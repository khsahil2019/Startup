# Startup Impact — Master UI Reference

The attached `docs/assets/Startup-Impact-UI-Master-Reference.png` is the master visual and information-architecture reference for the final Startup Impact interface.

## Required public experience
1. University Startup Impact Home: hero, institutional positioning, ecosystem statistics, quick navigation, featured startups.
2. Startup Directory: search, filters, startup cards, stage, sector, valuation, investability, AI resilience and SDG indicators.
3. Startup Profile: hero, founder, facts, KPI strip and tabbed intelligence profile.
4. Valuation tab: indicative pre-money valuation, range, methodology and factor contribution.
5. Road Ahead: five-year timeline, milestones and scenario financial projections.
6. Uniqueness: differentiation drivers and unique value proposition.
7. Investability: readiness score, dimensions, strengths and watch points.
8. AI Resilience: exposure, augmentation, data/IP moat, adaptability and governance.
9. SDG Impact: score, primary SDGs, dimensions and outcome metrics.
10. Clients & Revenue: clients, revenue, growth, geographies, segments and revenue trend.

The design is not a static mockup. Every visible value must come from the approved startup record, founder submission, evidence or derived engine output. Hard-coded demo content must never replace real submitted data in production.

## Visual language
- White/light institutional background.
- Deep navy/royal blue primary identity.
- Clean cards, subtle borders and restrained shadows.
- Large readable numbers and labels.
- SDG colours only for SDG-related indicators.
- Responsive desktop/tablet/mobile layouts.
- Premium University ecosystem feel rather than a generic admin dashboard.
