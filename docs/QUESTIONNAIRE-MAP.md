# Questionnaire-to-Intelligence Map

Founder & Journey -> founder execution/commitment signals.
Founder Context -> incubation support needs and resource-access context.
Startup & Problem -> problem quality and validation.
Product, Clients & Proof -> traction, customer evidence and revenue.
Market & Competition -> market opportunity and defensibility.
Team & Execution -> founder/team readiness and execution.
Business Model & Scale -> economics and scalability.
IP, Data & AI -> AI resilience and defensibility.
SDG & Impact -> SDG relevance, reach, measurement and centrality.
Funding, Road Ahead & Risk -> capital requirement and planning.

No public score should use sensitive personal information as a proxy for investment worth. Resource/context information is intended primarily for support planning.
